# Share and trait scale

## Question

In what units are party GDP shares and voter traits when they enter the score?

## Answer

Party GDP shares are values in `[0, 1]` and **the nine shares of a party must sum to 1**. Voter traits enter the matrix **raw** (e.g. `age` 45 vs `veteran` 1).
