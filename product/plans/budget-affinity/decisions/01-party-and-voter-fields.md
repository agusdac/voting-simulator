# Party and voter fields

## Question

Which GDP spend categories land on `PoliticalParty`, and which intrinsic voter traits land on `Voter`, linearly related to those categories?

## Done means

The declared v0.2 field names on party and voter are locked (English identifiers). Starting four party categories are health, education, infrastructure, defense unless this answer cuts them.

## Answer

Party GDP spend shares (all of them): `health`, `education`, `infrastructure`, `defense`, `social_protection`, `environment`, `housing`, `research`, `public_order`.

Voter uses intrinsic traits (option 2) plus `gender`. No `kids`: `age`, `dependents`, `urban`, `health_status`, `education_level`, `veteran`, `gender`. `dependents` is people the voter supports (including children).
